# Research revision v3.4.0

Theorem 7.61 proves that exterior centres can remove at most 23% of the annular volume left by the inner shell between radii \(\sqrt{3/2}\) and \(5/4\). An independent vertex-cone argument gives a uniform correction greater than \(1/28600\) to the truncated cell volume. Theorem 8.2 combines that correction with the cap certificates to bound the density of every four-dimensional unit-ball packing below \(0.634764\).

This revision also includes the tilted-shell volume theorem, the exact exterior-cut test, and the certified neighbourhoods of specific shells. The new diagrams and Appendix I give the geometric construction and rational comparisons. The unrestricted 24-neighbour case and the residual cases with 25–27 neighbours remain open.

The PDF and complete TeX sources are supplied both separately and in d424.zip. The new standard-library checks are included under anc/.
