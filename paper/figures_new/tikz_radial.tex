% tikz_radial.tex -- the proposition "Where the twenty-four centres lie": for |Y| = 24 and T(Y) <= 8,
% the number of centres beyond a radius rho.
%   (a) The cap budget alone, k(rho) = floor((24 S(2) - tau)/(S(2) - S(rho))), tau = 9 pi^2/8 - 8
%       (step curve, ball arithmetic), against the counts proved by the certificates (filled marks):
%       2 beyond 2.1 and 1 beyond 2.15, where the budget allows 3 and 2.
%   (b) For each split N(rho) <= c - 1 against N(rho) >= c, the bound on the union of the caps in the
%       first case: proved by the certificate (filled; c = 22 at 2.1, c = 23 at 2.15; radial_bounds.dat,
%       from radial_case_check.py), and the floating-point optimum of the programme of the same degree
%       for the next count (open; c = 23 at 2.1, c = 24 at 2.2 and 2.25; radial_next.dat), against the level
%       9 pi^2/8 - 8 that T > 8 needs.
% Data: radial_budget.dat (radial_data.py), radial_bounds.dat, radial_next.dat.
% Build:  pdflatex tikz_radial.tex && pdftoppm -r 500 -png -singlefile tikz_radial.pdf ../figures/fig_tikz_radial
% Test:   python3 tikz_overlap_check.py tikz_radial.tex
