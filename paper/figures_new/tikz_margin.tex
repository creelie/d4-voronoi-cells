% tikz_margin.tex -- the margin programme of the second level is spent by the root system
% (Proposition "The root system spends the margin").
%   (a) The weight w(u) = (u+1)(u+1/2)^2 u^2 (1/2+s-u) at s = 0.008, logarithmic scale, against
%       the floor 36 s = 0.288 that the 96 neighbouring pairs of the root system impose on
%       (N-24)/mu.  Every inner product lies below the floor: nothing is pinned.
%   (b) max w against 36 s as functions of s; the margin can pin anything only to the left of
%       their crossing, near s = 5.2e-4, far below the slack 0.008 of the stable kissing number.
% Data: margin_w.dat and margin_floor.dat, written by margin_data.py.
% Build:  pdflatex tikz_margin.tex && pdftoppm -r 500 -png -singlefile tikz_margin.pdf ../figures/fig_tikz_margin
