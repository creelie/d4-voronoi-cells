% tikz_secondorder.tex -- the second order of the cell volume at the root system
% (multi_cap/second_order).
%   (a) vol(V(Y)) - 8 against S = sum delta_i: the second-order model (2/3) S - (1/2) S^2, the
%       worst case on the first-order cone (certified); the exact value with one centre pushed
%       out, 1/3 - (1 - S/2)^4/3; and the exact value with all 24 pushed out evenly
%       (secondorder_even.dat, halfspace intersection); the dotted line is where the pair terms
%       of Lemma 2.15 reach 8 along the even push.
%   (b) vol(V(Y)) - 8 - (2/3) S + (1/2) S^2 on the 300 packings of remainder_test.py (tilts on
%       the edges of the cone, up to 5.8 S), against the one-centre ray, exactly
%       delta^3/6 - delta^4/48.  Floating point: evidence, not a bound.
