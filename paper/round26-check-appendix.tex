\section{Exact controls for angular stability}\label{round26:app:angular}
For the proof of Theorem~\ref{round26:thm:unequal-angular}, the exact endpoint values are
\[
 q(1/10)=\frac{227}{600}>\frac38,
 \qquad \frac38-\frac{1424}{12500}=\frac{6527}{25000}>\frac14.
\]
The twenty-four signed roots and their twenty-four dual facets have six incidences at each end. The check \texttt{anc/check\_exact.py} enumerates these incidences, verifies the Hadamard symmetry and checks the two rational coefficients. These finite checks support the written proof. The unrestricted localization and the remaining cases of (C) require further arguments.
