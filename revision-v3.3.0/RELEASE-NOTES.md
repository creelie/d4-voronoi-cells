# The Sphere Packing Problem in Dimension 4 and the Twenty-Four-Cell Conjecture

Complete integrated research revision v3.3.0, 211 pages.

The complete manuscript incorporates a quantitative angular stability theorem for unequal root push-outs, with an explicit packing example. The unrestricted cases (G) and (C) remain open.

The complete earlier manuscript, its active TeX inputs and figures are retained. The additions include diagrams, footnotes, detailed proofs, primary references and an appendix of exact controls. The new arguments have written proofs and finite checks. No new Lean formalization or journal-acceptance claim is made.
